Support

We’re here to help with anything about your Xself Home order or account. The fastest way to reach us is the in-app chat (Account → Support), or email us any time.

Orders, pickup & delivery

Questions about an order’s status, changing a warehouse pickup time, or a delivery — include your order number (it looks like XS-12345) so we can help faster.

Payments & refunds

Checkout is processed securely by trusted payment providers; we never store your full card details. For billing questions or refund requests, contact us with your order number and we’ll take care of it.

Account & data

You can manage your details, saved items, and orders in the app. To request access to or deletion of your account data, email us from the address on your account. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Email us

We typically reply within one business day.
